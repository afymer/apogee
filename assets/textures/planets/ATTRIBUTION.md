# Planet textures

`mercury.jpg`, `venus_atmosphere.jpg`, `mars.jpg`, `jupiter.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`, `uranus.jpg`,
`neptune.jpg` and `saturn_ring_alpha.png` are the 2K maps from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), derived from NASA imagery and
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The Saturn ring strip spans 74,500 km (C ring inner edge) to 140,220 km (F ring) from the planet's
center. Uranus' ring strip is generated procedurally in `planetarium/src/solar_system.rs`.
